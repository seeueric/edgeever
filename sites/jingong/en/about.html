Company

A company that builds industrial facilities

Jingong started in steel fabrication and added engineering, construction and maintenance over time. Our scope now runs from a single building to a complete industrial site.

Profile

Jingong Industrial Building Systems Group Co., Ltd. designs, fabricates and erects steel structures, modular building systems and metal envelope systems, and works as an EPC contractor on industrial projects.

We run our own engineering and detailing teams, our own fabrication plant and our own erection crews. Members are numbered through cutting, assembly, welding and coating; each leaves the shop with a quality record that matches the site erection log.

Our clients are mainly in automotive, new energy, electronics, logistics, food and pharmaceuticals, and equipment manufacturing.
