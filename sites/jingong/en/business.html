Capabilities

Capabilities

These six services can be taken on separately or bundled into a single EPC contract, with engineering, procurement and construction under one responsibility.

01 — 钢结构工程

Steel Structures

Design, fabrication and erection of single- and multi-storey industrial buildings, heavy bays and long-span structures. For high-seismic sites, heavy crane duty and long column-free spans we cover everything from system selection to connection detailing.

  • Portal framesEconomical and quick to build for standard single-storey plants and warehouses.
  • Heavy industrial baysSized for high-capacity cranes and heavy process equipment, with grid and foundations developed together.
  • Space structuresGrids, trusses and tensioned systems for arenas, hangars and terminals.
  • Coating & fire protectionSystems specified to the service environment and required fire rating, with inspection records retained.

02 — 装配式建筑系统

Modular Building Systems

Structure, envelope, services and fit-out are coordinated once at design stage; members are shop-fabricated and site work is mostly assembly. Programme time, site labour, dust and construction waste all drop against conventional methods.

  • Standard module libraryGrids, storey heights and connection details are held as reusable modules, cutting design and fabrication cost.
  • High shop contentFrames, floor decks and wall panels are completed in the shop; site work is connection and adjustment only.
  • Multiple building typesThe same system covers multi-storey plants, utility buildings, offices and staff accommodation.
  • Alterable and extendableStructural and services interfaces are left for future capacity or process changes.
03 — 金属围护系统

Metal Envelope Systems

Roof, wall, daylighting and drainage are designed and installed by one team. Detailing, material selection and sequence stay under single control, which avoids the leaks and condensation that come from unclear handovers between subcontractors.

  • Standing-seam roofsNon-penetrating fixings with wind clips to improve uplift resistance.
  • Insulation & vapour controlSpecified to local climate and internal conditions to control condensation risk.
  • Daylight & ventilationRooflights, ventilators and smoke extraction coordinated to code.
  • DetailingStandard details for gutters, parapets, movement joints and roof penetrations.
04 — 光伏建筑一体化

Building-Integrated PV

Panel loads, mounting, cable routes and access walkways are fixed during structural design, so a later retrofit does not run into insufficient roof capacity or damaged waterproofing details.

  • Load verificationAllowed for from the start on new build; a structural assessment is issued before retrofit on existing roofs.
  • Non-penetrating fixingStanding-seam roofs use purpose-made clamps, leaving the waterproofing layer intact.
  • DocumentationTechnical information prepared for green building and green factory submissions.
  • Access provisionCleaning, maintenance and fire access are planned into the panel layout.
05 — EPC 工程总承包

EPC Contracting

Engineering, procurement and construction sit with one company. The client does not coordinate between a design institute, suppliers and site crews — just one team and one contract.

  • Clear responsibilityTechnical, programme and quality accountability sit in one place.
  • Cost controlValue engineering starts at design stage, reducing later variations and overrun.
  • Parallel programmeDetailing, fabrication and site work progress in interlocking batches.
  • CompliancePermits, inspection records and as-built documentation are compiled and handed over.
06 — 检测与运维服务

Inspection & Maintenance

Buildings in service see load changes, process alterations, coating ageing and severe weather. We provide structural surveys, roof refurbishment, extensions and scheduled inspection for completed buildings.

  • Structural surveysDeformation, connections, corrosion and welds, reported with recommended action.
  • Coating re-checksFilm thickness and integrity checked, with local touch-up or full recoating.
  • Roof refurbishmentLeak remediation, insulation upgrades and PV retrofit handled together.
  • Extensions & alterationsAdded floors, extended spans and process openings for capacity increases.
