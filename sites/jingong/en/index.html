01 — Company

We build industrial facilities. The measures are plain: does it start production on the contract date, does it stay on budget, and is the roof still watertight in ten years.

We run our own engineering and detailing teams, our own fabrication plant and our own erection crews. Design, fabrication and site work sit with one party, which removes interface coordination and keeps responsibility for schedule and quality in one place.

Our scope covers steel structures, modular building systems, metal envelope systems, building-integrated photovoltaics, EPC contracting, and inspection and maintenance once the building is in use.

04 — Process

Five stages from brief to production

Each stage has a defined deliverable and a named contact.

01

Brief

Process flow, loads, site constraints and the target production date are captured as a requirements list.

02

Scheme & estimate

Structural options, general arrangement and a budget estimate, with the technical boundary and key assumptions stated.

03

Detailed design

BIM modelling, clash detection and connection detailing. Production scheduling starts once drawings are frozen.

04

Fabrication & erection

Shop fabrication runs in parallel with site erection, inspected batch by batch, with photographic records at key holds.

05

Handover & service

Completion, document handover and operator briefing, followed by scheduled inspection and maintenance.
