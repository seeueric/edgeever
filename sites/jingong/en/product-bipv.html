PV Roof Systems光伏屋面系统

Photovoltaics are allowed for during structural design — panel loads, mounting, cable routes and access are fixed once, not worked around after completion.

01

System range

01 Integrated new build (BIPV) Panel loads and cable routes are counted at structural design stage and purlin spacing optimised to the panel layout, so roof and PV are completed in one go.
02 Retrofit on existing roofs A structural assessment is issued first, confirming roof capacity, purlin spacing and sheet profile — with a strengthening scheme where they fall short.
03 Non-penetrating fixing Purpose-made clamps on standing seam roofs leave the sheet and the waterproofing layer intact. On retrofit work this is the single most important detail.
04 Access provision Cleaning, maintenance and fire access are planned into the panel layout, so the array stays serviceable after completion.

03

Materials & standards

  • Building PVGB/T 51368 Technical standard for application of building photovoltaic systems.
  • PV generationGB 50797 Code for design of photovoltaic power stations (grid connection and electrical).
  • Load verificationGB 50009 Load code, with panel loads added to roof permanent loads.
  • RoofingGB 50345 Technical code for roof engineering — a retrofit must not reduce the existing waterproofing grade.
  • Grid connectionConnection arrangements follow the local grid operator; capacity and self-consumption ratio confirmed up front.

Standard references must be checked against the current valid editions before publication — standards are revised, and an out-of-date reference gets picked up at tender.

04

Performance & options

  • Operating modeSelf-consumption with export, full export, or off-grid with storage, selected on local policy and the site load profile.
  • StrengtheningWhere the assessment fails, we provide purlin infill, secondary beams or sheet replacement options.
  • Green submissionsTechnical documentation prepared for green building, green factory and carbon reduction submissions.
