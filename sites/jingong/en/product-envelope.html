Products & Systems

Metal Envelope Systems金属围护系统

Roof, wall, daylighting and drainage from one team. The envelope decides whether the building is still dry and condensation-free in ten years.

01

System range

01 Standing seam roof system Non-penetrating fixing with seamed ribs and wind clips — the most consistent of the four for uplift resistance and weathertightness.
02 Wall systems Horizontally or vertically laid profiled sheet or sandwich panels, with insulation and liner, selected on fire compartment and appearance requirements.
03 Daylight & ventilation Rooflights, ventilators and smoke extraction coordinated to code, with rooflight area sized to the required working illuminance.
04 Drainage & detailing Standard details for gutters, parapets, movement joints and penetrations, with capacity checked against local rainfall intensity.

03

Materials & standards

  • RoofingGB 50345 Technical code for roof engineering; pitched roofs additionally GB 50693.
  • Profiled sheetGB 50896 Technical code for application of profiled metal sheet.
  • Wind upliftUplift testing to ASTM E1592 or UL 580; in coastal and high-wind areas verify against measured wind pressures.
  • LoadingGB 50009 Load code for building structures, with wind loads to the local reference pressure.
  • FireInsulation reaction-to-fire class selected to the building fire compartment requirements and current external wall regulations.

Standard references must be checked against the current valid editions before publication — standards are revised, and an out-of-date reference gets picked up at tender.

04

Performance & options

  • Sheet materialAluzinc-coated steel, pre-painted steel or aluminium-magnesium-manganese; higher coating grades for coastal exposure.
  • Insulation & vapour controlLayer thickness and vapour barrier position set by local climate and internal conditions to control cold bridging and condensation.
  • Uplift configurationClip spacing tightened separately in corner, edge and field zones.
  • AirtightnessLap and flashing details specified to the airtightness target, with blower-door and thermal imaging checks available before handover.
