Products & Systems

Modular Building Systems装配式建筑系统

Grids, storey heights and connection details are held as reusable modules. Structure, floors and walls are completed in the shop; site work is mostly assembly.

01

System range

01 Standard module library Grids, storey heights and connection details are fixed as reusable modules, called up directly at scheme stage to cut repeat design and shop tooling changes.
02 Composite floor systems Profiled steel composite decks or steel-truss deck, installed without formwork alongside the main frame.
03 Prefabricated wall systems Sandwich or precast panels with insulation, finish and openings completed in the shop; site work is fixing and sealing only.
04 Services provision Cable routes, equipment bases and openings are located once during detailing, avoiding later cutting of the structure.

03

Materials & standards

  • System standardGB/T 51232 Technical standard for prefabricated steel structure buildings.
  • Prefabrication ratingGB/T 51129 Assessment standard for prefabricated buildings, supporting client submissions.
  • Floor decksComposite decks designed to the relevant clauses of GB 50017, with deck profiles to product standards.
  • Structural designGB 50017 and GB 50011.
  • ExecutionGB 50205 acceptance code for steel structure construction.

Standard references must be checked against the current valid editions before publication — standards are revised, and an out-of-date reference gets picked up at tender.

04

Performance & options

  • Prefabrication ratioModule scope set to the project target, covering structure, floors, walls and some services.
  • Wall optionsSandwich panels, precast concrete panels or curtain wall, selected on fire and appearance requirements.
  • ExtendableStructural and services interfaces are left for later floors or extended spans without breaking into the existing frame.
  • DemountablePredominantly bolted modules can be dismantled and reassembled, suited to leased or temporary facilities.
