Products & Systems

Steel Building Systems钢结构系统

Built around the portal frame, extending through multi-storey frames, heavy industrial bays and long-span space structures. All four share the same detailing, traceable fabrication and erection process.

01

System range

01 Portal frame system Tapered solid-web frames, economical between 18–36 m span at 6–9 m bay spacing. For single-storey plants, warehousing and logistics — the lowest steel tonnage per m² of the four.
02 Multi-storey frame system Rigid or pinned beam-column frames with composite floor decks, 4.5–6 m storey heights. For multi-storey plants, R&D buildings and utility blocks; combines with modular floor and wall panels.
03 Heavy industrial system Designed for high-capacity cranes and heavy process equipment, 9–12 m bays and eaves up to 30 m. Crane girders and bracing are separately checked for fatigue.
04 Space frame system Grids, tubular trusses and tensioned systems from 40–120 m span. For arenas, hangars and terminals, usually installed by synchronous lifting or sectional erection.

03

Materials & standards

  • SteelGB/T 700 carbon structural steel and GB/T 1591 low-alloy high-strength steel. Q235B and Q355B are standard; Q390 and above for heavy or long-span members.
  • Structural designGB 50017 Standard for design of steel structures; portal frames additionally to GB 51022.
  • Seismic designGB 50011 Code for seismic design of buildings, to the intensity of the project location.
  • WeldingGB 50661 Code for welding of steel structures; welding quality management can run to ISO 3834.
  • Corrosion protectionSurface preparation to GB/T 8923; coating system selected for the service environment and design life.
  • Fire protectionGB 51249 Technical code for fire safety of steel structures, with intumescent or board protection to the required rating.
  • ExecutionGB 50205 Code for acceptance of construction quality of steel structures.
  • Export projectsCan be designed and fabricated to EN 1993 (Eurocode 3) with EN 1090 execution classes, or to AISC 360. Scope of application is set by contract.

Standard references must be checked against the current valid editions before publication — standards are revised, and an out-of-date reference gets picked up at tender.

04

Performance & options

  • Coating systemsHot-dip galvanising, or zinc-rich primer with epoxy intermediate and polyurethane topcoat; specified to C3/C4/C5 corrosivity categories.
  • Fire protectionThin- or thick-film intumescent coatings, or board encasement; rated at 1.0 / 1.5 / 2.0 / 3.0 hours.
  • ConnectionsPredominantly high-strength bolted, with site welding where required. Connection design is fixed during detailing.
  • TraceabilityMembers are numbered from cutting onward and shipped with mill certificates, weld inspection records and coating thickness reports.
  • Crane provisionSingle- and double-girder overhead cranes, typically 5–100 t; heavier duty designed to the process requirement.
